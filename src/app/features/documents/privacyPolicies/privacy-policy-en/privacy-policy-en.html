2. Basic Terms

Documented information – information recorded on a physical medium through documentation, together with details that allow such information or its physical medium to be identified.

Information – data (notices, information) regardless of the form of its presentation.

Processing of personal data – any action (operation) or set of actions (operations) performed using automation tools or without the use of such tools with personal data, including collection, recording, systematization, accumulation, storage, clarification (updating, modification), extraction, use, transfer (dissemination, provision, access), deletion, and destruction of personal data.

Contractor – a person who, independently or jointly with other persons, organizes and/or carries out the processing of personal data, and also determines the purposes of processing personal data, the composition of personal data to be processed, and the actions (transactions) performed with personal data.

Personal data – any information relating directly or indirectly to a specific natural person (subject of personal data), including: the User's surname, first name, patronymic, email address(es), phone number(s), as well as any other data directly or indirectly relating to a specific or identifiable natural person and identifying the subject of personal data, which may be obtained in the course of providing services, as well as other information disclosed by the user on the basis of which identification of the subject of personal data is possible.

User – a person who, using technical devices, gains access to this Web Resource.

Destruction of personal data – actions as a result of which it becomes impossible to restore the content of personal data in an information system and/or as a result of which the physical media of personal data are destroyed.
