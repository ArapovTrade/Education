Privacy Policy

1. Who processes personal data

Sole Proprietor Ihor Vitaliyovych Arapov (the Contractor) operates arapov.education and determines why personal data is processed for its services.

The Contractor’s address and details are listed in the Public Offer Agreement. For questions about personal data, email arapov.trade@gmail.com.

The Contractor follows Ukrainian law, including the Law of Ukraine On Personal Data Protection, No. 2297-VI of June 1, 2010.

2. What data is used and where it comes from

  • Inquiries. Your name or username, contact details, message, and any materials you send by email or messenger.
  • Orders and payments. The format you select, contact details, order information, and payment confirmation received through WayForPay or from you when clarifying the order.
  • Access and training. The email address of the Google Account you provide for access to private lessons, information needed to arrange classes, and any coursework you submit for feedback or review.
  • Refunds and complaints. Information needed to verify the order, request, and payment. If a refund to the original payment method cannot be made, the Contractor may request details for the agreed alternative refund method.
  • Website visits. When you load pages and connected services, your browser sends the technical information needed for the network request. Ahrefs Web Analytics provides website traffic statistics.

3. Why data is processed and on what basis

The Contractor processes the data needed to answer course inquiries you initiate, place and confirm orders, provide access, arrange classes, review coursework, and offer support. This processing is based on steps taken at your request before entering into a contract and on performing that contract.

Payment and refund records may be used for accounting and to meet legal obligations. Data may also be processed for the Contractor’s legitimate interests, including website security, traffic measurement, and handling complaints, subject to the priority of your rights and freedoms.

Where processing requires consent, the Contractor asks for it for the specific purpose. Visiting the website, following a link, or asking for information does not constitute consent to receive promotional messages.

4. External services and technical data

  • WayForPay processes payments. You enter payment details on its checkout page. The Contractor uses the order and payment confirmation information available to it to provide services and process refunds.
  • Google and YouTube provide access to private videos through the Google Account you give us. YouTube also provides the player for public videos on the website. Google processes data under its own policies when these features are used.
  • Zoom is used for classes held on that platform. Participation data is processed under Zoom’s policies.
  • Telegram and WhatsApp let you contact the Contractor outside the website. The Contractor uses the correspondence it receives to answer questions or provide support.
  • Cloudflare supports website delivery and security. Your browser also contacts Google when loading fonts from Google Fonts.
  • Ahrefs Web Analytics is used to measure website visits. According to Ahrefs’ documentation, the tool works without cookies.

External services may use cookies and process technical data under their own policies. This depends on the service, the features you use, and your browser or account settings. If providing a service requires transferring personal data outside Ukraine, the Contractor follows Ukrainian law on such transfers.

5. Retention and security

The Contractor retains data for as long as needed for the relevant purpose. The period depends on how long the service lasts, the need to keep transaction records and resolve inquiries and complaints, and legal requirements. Data is deleted or anonymized once a valid basis for processing no longer exists.

The Contractor uses organizational and technical measures to protect personal data. Access is limited to tasks that require that information.

6. Communications and your rights

Messages about orders, access, classes, payments, and support are part of providing the services. The Contractor sends promotional messages only with separate, voluntary consent. You can withdraw consent by emailing arapov.trade@gmail.com.

You can request information about processing and access to your personal data, ask to correct inaccurate data, object to processing, or request deletion where there are legal grounds. You can withdraw consent where processing is based on consent.

Send requests to arapov.trade@gmail.com. You also have the right to lodge a complaint with the Ukrainian Parliament Commissioner for Human Rights or take the matter to court.

7. Changes to this Privacy Policy

The current version is published on this page. If the law requires consent for a new processing purpose, the Contractor asks for it separately.
