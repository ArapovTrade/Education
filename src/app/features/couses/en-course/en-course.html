Let's talk practice

This course
is right for you if:

You want to start from scratch — the right way

Most beginners lose money not because of the market, but because of a chaotic start: scattered videos, conflicting advice, and trading "by feel." The course gives you a clear structure: from how the exchange works to a complete trading system, step by step, with no gaps in your knowledge.

You've tried before but aren't seeing results

If you've already placed trades, studied indicators and strategies, but still don't have consistent results — the issue is almost never a lack of knowledge, it's a lack of a system. On the course we'll break down your own approach and rebuild it around volume analysis and the logic of large capital.

You're ready to move to systematic trading

You want to stop guessing and start understanding: why price moves the way it does, and what the market's major players are doing. The course gives you a ready-made trading system with a positive mathematical expectation — plus practice on real charts to make it stick.

Our teaching approach:

Depth, practice, and personal attention

01

The course is made up of 10 in-depth lessons

5 sessions are concentrated theory that lays a solid foundation — no filler, no generic advice, only what you actually need for trading. The next 5 sessions are 100% practice, where you work the live market side by side with a mentor: finding entry points, tracking trades, and reviewing them in real time. We don't just explain, we show and do it together, until the system clicks for you personally.

02

You don't just learn — you prove the skill

Knowledge that's never tested is quickly forgotten, so after the theory block you take an exam on the material, and after the practice block a trading exam on a demo account: 40 trades, strictly by the system's rules. This isn't a formality — it's how we confirm you can actually apply the system, not just sit through the lessons. The course ends not with knowledge in your head, but a working skill in your hands.

03

Personal work with Igor Arapov

Training runs in mini-groups of up to 6 people — a deliberate limit, not a marketing trick. That group size is exactly what lets me personally lead every cohort: review each participant's actual trades, answer questions without delay, and make sure no one is left behind on a topic. There's no "one of a thousand" feeling here: just a mentor who sees your trading and helps you build it.

Module 1

Foundations: How markets work

We break down how the exchange works from the inside: who the market participants are (market makers, institutional and retail traders), how price is formed, and why price movement isn't random — it's the result of orders colliding with one another. You'll get the base without which any technical or volume analysis is done blind.

Module 2

Technical analysis: reading the chart

You'll learn to identify trend direction, build strong support and resistance levels, and recognize reversal and continuation patterns. This is the market's language — after this module you'll stop seeing the chart as a random pile of candles.

Module 3

Volume analysis: market mechanics

This is where the difference between a systematic trader and a beginner begins. We dig into order-matching mechanics and cluster volume analysis to understand how large market participants operate. You'll learn to see the real balance of supply and demand on the chart — and spot where large capital is actually positioned.

Module 4

Trading system: one unified plan of action

We bring everything together into a single trading system with clear rules: when to enter a trade, how to manage it, and exactly when to exit — with no second-guessing and no emotional decisions. This isn't a collection of indicators — it's an algorithm with a positive mathematical expectation that you'll be able to apply on your own after the course.
