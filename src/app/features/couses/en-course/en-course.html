Our approach to learning:

Depth, practice, and personal attention

01

The course consists of 10 in-depth lessons

5 lessons are concentrated theory that builds a solid foundation. The next 5 lessons are 100% practice, where you work in the market side by side with a mentor. We don't just tell you — we show you and do it together.

02

You don't just learn — you prove your skill

After the theory you take a knowledge exam, and after the practice — a trading exam on a demo account, making 40 trades by the system's rules. You finish the course with a practical skill and an understanding of the trading system.

03

Personal work with Igor Arapov

Training takes place in mini-groups of up to 6 people. This is not a "flow" for hundreds of participants. Igor Arapov personally leads each group, answers all questions, and reviews your trades.
